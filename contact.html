Before you visit

Quick answers to common questions.

How long does a typical repair take?

Many common repairs such as battery, keyboard and RAM or SSD upgrades are completed the same day. Board-level work and data recovery take longer, and we give you an estimate after diagnosis.

Do I pay for the diagnosis?

We explain the issue and share a quote before any repair work starts. You approve the quote first, so nothing is done without your agreement.

Will my data be safe?

We back up your files before software work and treat your data as private. For hardware repairs we avoid touching your files unless it is needed for the fix.

Do you use genuine parts?

Yes. We use genuine or quality-tested components, and we tell you exactly what is going into your device.

Is there a warranty on repairs?

Completed repairs are covered by a service warranty so you can ask us to look again if the same fault returns.
